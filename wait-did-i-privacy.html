Privacy Policy

Wait, Did I? is built to be local-first. The app is designed without user accounts, ads, analytics, third-party tracking, or data sale.

Information the app stores

Wait, Did I? stores the content and preferences you create inside the app, including the routines and checklist items you add, whether each item is currently confirmed and when, your record of resisted re-check urges, reminder settings, appearance theme, and onboarding status.

Where that information stays

This information is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to a server and is not shared with the developer. Your routines and progress remain private to you on your device.

Information collected by the developer

The app does not collect personal information from you and does not transmit your routines or progress to the developer. If you contact support, the information you choose to send in that message may be used to respond to your request.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Wait, Did I? does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies. It does not sell personal data.

Purchases

Wait, Did I? offers an optional one-time in-app purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The app does not receive or store your payment information; Apple handles the transaction under Apple's own policies.

Notifications

If you enable an optional routine reminder, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. You can change or turn them off at any time in the app and in your device's system settings.

Backups and Apple services

Your device settings, Apple account settings, iCloud backup settings, crash diagnostics settings, and App Store activity may be handled by Apple according to Apple's own policies. Wait, Did I? does not control those Apple services.

Children's privacy

Wait, Did I? is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health and wellness note

Wait, Did I? is a self-help organizer for people who repeatedly re-check things. It is not medical care, mental health treatment, or a substitute for a qualified professional. The most effective help for checking that affects your life is Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) with a licensed therapist. If you are in crisis, please contact a crisis line or emergency services.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. If data practices change materially, this page should be updated before the new version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email support.altaaffirmations@gmail.com.
