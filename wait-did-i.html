What it does

Check it once. Then let it go.

Your own routines

Add exactly what you check, in your own words. Leaving the house, bedtime, the car, closing up.

Tap once, all clear

Confirm each thing with a single tap. The moment everything's checked, a calm "all clear" says so.

A gentle pause

Come back to re-check and the app offers a short pause instead of instant reassurance, so the doubt can settle on its own.

Progress that counts down

It tallies the urges you resist, not the times you check. Checking less is the win.

Ready-made templates

Start from a common walkthrough instead of a blank screen, then tweak it to fit your day. A few essentials are free, with more in Plus.

It clears itself

Marks reset at the end of each day, so the same list is ready tomorrow. Plus adds custom reset timers on your own schedule.

Why it's different

Most reassurance apps make checking easier. This one helps you need it less.

Wait, Did I? Plus

The free app is a complete checklist. Plus lifts the ceiling.

Two routines, the gentle pause, and your all-clear are free. For people who lean on it every day, an optional one-time Plus opens unlimited routines, custom reset timers, four more calming themes, and your full progress history over time. It is a one-time purchase, never a subscription.

Privacy

What you check stays yours.

Wait, Did I? is built local-first, with no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Your routines and progress live on your device. It's a self-help organizer, never a substitute for therapy or professional care.
