What it does

Small reminders with a personal center.

Custom-first affirmations

Write the exact line you need and save it locally in the category where it belongs.

40 affirmation packs

Everyday categories and hard-season packs, with 20 to 50 built-in lines each.

Widget rotation

Choose which categories feed the widget and whether lines stay fixed or refresh over time. Tap one and the app opens to exactly that affirmation.

Read before you unlock

Every locked pack opens far enough to read a few real lines, so you can tell whether it's for you before spending anything.

Favorites

Heart any line to keep it. Saved words follow you across the app, the widget, and your watch.

Search

Find the right words fast, across every pack and every line you have written.

Today

One affirmation chosen for the day, drawn from the packs you picked.

Daily reminders

Gentle notifications at the times that actually help, not on someone else's schedule.

Share cards

Turn any line into a soft gradient card and send it to someone who needs it.

Apple Watch

Your packs on your wrist, offline, with no phone required.

Love-note corner

Add an optional sweetheart name so the app can hold a little pocket of personal reassurance.

Opt-in bold language

Savager lines stay off until enabled, with word-by-word control for what can appear.

Themes & dark mode

Five calming palettes, each with a matching dark mode. Blush is free; Serene, Meadow, Golden, and Twilight come with Plus.

Tone

Gentle when you need gentle. Punchy when you ask for punchy.

Privacy

Your words stay yours.

Alta's Affirmations is designed without accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. Custom affirmations and settings are stored on your device and shared locally with the widget extension.
