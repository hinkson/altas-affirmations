In crisis right now?

Not Beaten is a companion, not emergency care. If you're in crisis, please reach out for real-time help — in the US you can call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), and the app links to online meetings and hotlines on its Resources screen.

Quick answers

Where are my journal and day count stored?
Everything is stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a server or to the developer.
Does the app require an account?
No. Not Beaten is designed without accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.
What happens to my badges after a hard day?
They stay. Badges are yours forever — logging a setback resets your day count kindly, but never erases what you've earned.
Can I change or turn off the daily reminder?
Yes. The evening gratitude reminder can be eased back to a lighter cadence or switched off entirely in Settings.
