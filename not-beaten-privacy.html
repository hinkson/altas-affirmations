Privacy Policy

Not Beaten is built to be local-first. The app is designed without user accounts, ads, analytics, third-party tracking, or data sale.

Information the app stores

Not Beaten stores the content and preferences you create inside the app, including your recovery start date and day count, your daily gratitudes and journal entries, any setbacks you log along with their optional private notes, earned milestone badges, reminder settings, appearance theme, and onboarding status.

Where that information stays

This information is stored locally on your device. It is not sent to a server and is not shared with the developer. Your journal, gratitudes, and recovery history remain private to you on your device.

Information collected by the developer

The app does not collect personal information from you and does not transmit your journal, gratitudes, or recovery data to the developer. If you contact support, the information you choose to send in that message may be used to respond to your request.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Not Beaten does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or tracking technologies. It does not sell personal data.

Notifications

If you enable the optional gratitude reminder, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. You can change how often they appear or turn them off at any time in Settings, and in your device's system settings.

Backups and Apple services

Your device settings, Apple account settings, iCloud backup settings, crash diagnostics settings, and App Store activity may be handled by Apple according to Apple's own policies. Not Beaten does not control those Apple services.

Children's privacy

Not Beaten is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Health and wellness note

Not Beaten is a self-care and reflection companion for people in recovery. It is not medical care, mental health treatment, addiction treatment, crisis support, or a substitute for a sponsor or a qualified professional. If you are in crisis, please contact a crisis line or emergency services.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. If data practices change materially, this page should be updated before the new version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email support.notbeaten@gmail.com.
